# Applied Chemistry, Physics & Biology 3D Simulations

Interactive 3D web simulations built with **Three.js** and **Tailwind CSS** designed with a **Hybrid Architecture**: separating foundational **Chemistry**, **Physics & Material Mechanics**, and **Biology** while highlighting cross-disciplinary connections.

## Included Simulations

1. **3D Periodic Table & Matter Simulation (`periodic-table-3d.html`)**
   - All 118 elements with 3D Bohr shells and Quantum Electron Clouds (`s, p, d, f` orbitals).
   - 3D Chemical Reaction Sandbox.
   - 8 Physical & Advanced Material Showcases (including Memory Foam viscoelastic compression, Nitinol shape-memory alloy, Diamond/Graphene, Silica Aerogel, YBCO Superconductor, 2nm GAAFET chip) & 4 Economic Industry Sectors.
2. **3D Plant Biosphere & Bio-Materials (`plant-biosphere-3d.html`)**
   - Xylem & Phloem vascular transport, stomatal dynamics with live sunlight & soil moisture controls.
   - C3, C4, and CAM photosynthetic adaptations.
   - Ecological VOCs, HIPVs defense signaling, and Mycorrhizal "Wood Wide Web".
   - 500-Million-Year evolutionary timeline (Bryophytes -> Ferns -> Gymnosperms -> Angiosperms).
   - Human industrial applications: wood pulp paper, natural dyes (Indigo, Curcumin), essential oils, and phytopharmaceuticals.
3. **3D DNA Double Helix & Embryo Cleavage (`dna-embryo-3d.html`)**
   - Atomic-scale B-DNA geometry (`A=T` 2 H-bonds, `G≡C` 3 H-bonds, Major/Minor grooves, Helicase unwinding).
   - 8-stage fertilization & embryonic cleavage across 4 animal groups: Mammals/Human, Amphibians (Frog), Avian (Bird), and Sea Urchin.

## Deploying to GitHub Pages

1. Upload all files (`index.html`, `periodic-table-3d.html`, `plant-biosphere-3d.html`, `dna-embryo-3d.html`, `README.md`) to the root of your GitHub repository.
2. Go to **Settings -> Pages** in your repository.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch** -> `main` / `/ (root)` and save.
